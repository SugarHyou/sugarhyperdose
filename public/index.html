What's your absolute favorite candy?
This is the most difficult question in the world... 'Cause sometimes I really like Oreos, y'know? But then other days I CRAVE Sour Patch Watermelon... I'd have to pick Oreos!
👤
What art program do you use? Brush? Animating software?
I use Ibispaint! I had IbisPaint X for 3 years before deciding to finally get the actual OG app...!
I mostly use a brush called "Pixel Perfect", but honestly the default rough pen and fade pen are extremely nice, too.
As for animation... I just draw every frame in Ibispaint, then go to EZGif and put 'em all together and hope for the best!
Professional animation software is too confusing for me.
